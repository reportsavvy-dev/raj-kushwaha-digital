# Raj Kushwaha Digital operating brief

## Shared responsibility

Raj Kushwaha owns lead generation, sales conversations and client approvals. Codex owns the website delivery system: technical SEO, AEO/GEO readiness, structured data, content architecture, internal linking, accessibility, performance checks and release QA.

## Non-negotiables

- Never invent client results, testimonials, certifications, partnerships or citations.
- Separate verified portfolio evidence from concepts and planning examples.
- Prefer current primary sources such as Google Search Central, Bing Webmaster, OpenAI documentation, standards bodies and platform documentation.
- Structured data must match visible page content. Do not add review stars or unsupported LocalBusiness details.
- Every indexable page needs a unique title, description, canonical URL, one clear H1 and useful internal links.
- New articles must answer a real buyer question, show practical judgment, cite primary sources and connect to a relevant service or case study.
- Preserve mobile usability and reduced-motion support. Do not trade Core Web Vitals for decorative animation.

## Publishing workflow

1. Confirm the search intent and business purpose.
2. Review the current SERP and first-party guidance when the topic is time-sensitive.
3. Draft a direct answer, supporting explanation, decision criteria, examples and source notes.
4. Add author, Article and breadcrumb data where appropriate.
5. Link the article to one service, one proof page when relevant and one next-step page.
6. Run build, tests, HTML checks and a production smoke test before release.
7. Record the publish date and review date in the content backlog.

## Measurement

Track qualified enquiries, assisted conversions, non-brand clicks, landing-page engagement and cited/linked appearances. Rankings and AI citations are observations, not guarantees.

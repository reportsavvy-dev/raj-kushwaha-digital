# Organic growth operating system

Last updated: 12 August 2026

## Roles

- Raj: outreach, discovery calls, proposal feedback, customer language and commercial priorities.
- Website and growth support: technical SEO, schema, content briefs, publishing, internal links, performance QA and reporting interpretation.

## 90-day focus

### Weeks 1 to 2: eligibility and measurement

- Keep important pages crawlable and included in the XML sitemap.
- Maintain Organization, Person, Service, Article and Breadcrumb schema that reflects visible content.
- Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster coverage after each material release.
- Review Core Web Vitals by template, especially the media-heavy homepage.

### Weeks 3 to 6: commercial topic clusters

Publish one evidence-led article per week. Start with:

1. SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO differences for business owners.
2. AI search citation eligibility and crawler controls.
3. B2B LinkedIn content measurement without vanity metrics.
4. SEO and LinkedIn planning for staffing and recruitment brands.
5. Search demand for medical billing and revenue-cycle companies.
6. Choosing between SEO, PPC and a combined acquisition plan.

Each article should link to a relevant service, a real case or work sample when available, the author profile and the contact page.

### Weeks 7 to 12: authority and conversion

- Refresh service pages with questions heard during sales calls.
- Publish one method or teardown based on anonymised, permission-safe client learning.
- Add new verified results only when source evidence and dates are available.
- Improve pages with impressions but weak clicks before creating more pages on the same intent.
- Compare form enquiries, assisted conversions and sales-qualified leads by landing page.

## Editorial standard

A useful article begins with a concise answer, then explains the decision. It names limits, dates unstable facts, uses concrete examples and links to primary guidance. It does not pad word count, repeat the same definition under multiple headings or manufacture certainty.

## Monthly review

- Google Search Console: indexed pages, non-brand queries, clicks, impressions and Core Web Vitals.
- Bing Webmaster Tools: indexed pages, crawl issues and AI Performance citations when available.
- Analytics: engaged sessions, contact starts, submissions and assisted conversions by landing page.
- Content: pages to update, consolidate or remove; new questions from outreach and calls.

AI platforms decide what they crawl, retrieve and cite. The controllable work is clear access, reliable entities, quotable explanations, visible evidence and a website people can use comfortably.
